Questions

Frequently asked questions

Hours, minimums, turnaround and prices.

What are your hours, and when is someone there?

The laundromat is open every day from 5 AM to 11 PM for self-serve washing and drying. An attendant is on site for wash & fold, dry cleaning and pickups during attendant hours: Mon, Wed, Thu and Fri 10 AM – 5 PM and 7 – 11 PM; Tue 7 – 11 PM; Sat 9 AM – 11 PM; Sun 10 AM – 11 PM.

Is there a minimum for wash & fold?

Yes, 10 lbs. Wash & fold is $1.80/lb and fold only is $1.00/lb. HST applies.

How long does wash & fold take? Can I get it the same day?

Your laundry is ready in 1 day. Same-day fast service is available as an add-on; ask us for the price.

Do you pick up and deliver?

Yes. Pickup and delivery is free within 2 km of the store for orders of 20 lbs or more. Call or email during attendant hours to book, or use the pickup request form.

How much does dry cleaning cost?

Prices start at $3.45 per item for a laundered dress shirt. The full price list covers shirts, suits, coats, dresses, linens, curtains, carpets and more, including wedding dresses. Prices are effective Jun 01, 2026, and HST applies to all prices.

Do you do alterations?

Yes. Ask in store for pricing.

Are you open on holidays?

Holiday hours may differ. Call 437-423-3962 to check before you come.

Can I do my own laundry there? Is there an ATM?

Yes. The self-serve coin-operated washers and dryers are open every day, 5 AM – 11 PM, and there is an ATM on site. Ask in store for machine prices.
